# Case studies → product decisions

Checked 2026-10-05. These are primary vendor descriptions and a Brex-authored press release, not independent evidence of usability, financial benefit or scientific novelty. Lessons below are design inferences to test with SMEs.

| Case / source | Observed evidence | Lesson adopted | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Brex SVB emergency credit announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brex-to-offer-emergency-credit-line-to-silicon-valley-bank-customers-to-meet-operational-spending-needs-301769414.html), 2023-03-10 | Brex described a bridge credit line for payroll and operational spending when startups could not access funds. This was an offer, not proof that funds were delivered. | Put essential obligations and first unmet day at the center of the task. | CascadeGuard models preparation using existing balances; it does not offer credit. The announcement does not establish SME prevalence or effectiveness. |
| [Mercury Vault announcement](https://mercury.com/blog/mercury-vault-announcement), 2023-03-12 | Mercury described dashboard suggestions and an overview of funds across institutions. Its offering included sweep and investment facilities. | Connect the result to a reviewable action and retain user control. | We adopt the interaction idea. We do not inherit Mercury's protections, investment products, current insurance terms or assurances of uninterrupted access. |
| [Float Scenario Planning help](https://intercom.help/floatapp/en/articles/1719419-scenario-planning), page dated 2026-07-10 | Float describes a base forecast and scenario layers that add, change or remove budgets. | Keep before/after visible and preserve the baseline until confirmation. | CascadeGuard uses existing funds and 30-day obligations; it excludes future receipts and accounting integrations. Vendor descriptions are not independent usability studies. |

## Candidate differentiation to validate

One focused interruption-preparation task with cent-exact minimum preparation to a user-selected destination, reproducible local calculations, reviewed application and portable input/history. This is a proposed product positioning, not a claim that no competitor has these features.

## Next evidence

Interview US SME owners/treasury operators about their current contingency workflow. Measure task completion, time, comprehension and whether the plan helps them identify a concrete preparation step. Expand features only if observed failures or repeated needs justify it. No study results are available yet.
